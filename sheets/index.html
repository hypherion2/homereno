16 – Kitchen landing

Est. cost $157k–292k CAD · Concept 16 · open in 3D

Concept 15 with the stairs turning into the kitchen. At the landing the stair now turns right on winders toward the kitchen, so you arrive in the open area by the south end of the island, facing the lounge, with a direct route from the garage and front door up to the kitchen. Upper floor: the wall between the hall and the kitchen comes out (it is probably bearing, so it needs an engineered beam), the double-sided fireplace chase and the dry-pantry run go, and the old stair opening is floored over. A single-sided gas fireplace moves to the lounge west wall with two fireside chairs; the TV stays in the oak nook. Ground floor: the flat landing becomes three winders that make the right turn, so the upper flight is short and stops before the garage; the garage keeps its square corner and the door from the mud-room vestibule keeps full headroom. The space under the old flight and the landing becomes a storage closet off the foyer. Most expensive option: beam, new floor opening, gas line and permits.

Stair to kitchen · Winder turn · Bearing wall: beam · Garage untouched

17 – Compact stair (archived)

Est. cost $157k–331k CAD · Concept 17 · open in 3D

Concept 15 with the stair moved west into a compact L that lands in the kitchen. The diagonal switchback is replaced by a compact L-stair (two 100 cm flights, 6 + 8 steps, 18 cm rise and 26 cm treads) set against the east side of the foyer. The partition between the foyer and the office moves 20 cm west (the office door stays) to make room for the full-width stair. You climb north from the front door, turn right at a square landing and the upper flight runs east along the north wall, arriving in the kitchen about 1.2 m from the island stools. Ground floor: the route from the front door to the laundry and to the hall beside it stays about 1 m wide past the foot of the stair, and the utility room is reached through the existing hall door into a 1 m passage (with a shallow linen cupboard). The old stairwell becomes a walk-in bulk pantry off the mud-room vestibule, right by the garage entry (about 3 m², about 1.8 m² of it full height): shelving, low shelves and a chest freezer under the top of the stair. Upstairs, a 1.6 m wall of full-height pull-out pantry cabinets faces the island for everyday food. Upper floor: the wall between the hall and the kitchen comes out (beam needed), the hall to the west bedrooms widens to about 1.5 m and the old stairwell becomes floor that joins the lounge. Needs an engineer for the beam and the new floor opening; the walls under the top steps are cut down to the stair soffit.

Compact L-stair · Lands in the kitchen · Storage room · Arrive in the kitchen

18 – Corner winder

Est. cost $142k–291k CAD · Concept 18 · open in 3D

Concept 16 with the stair turning straight into the kitchen on a single corner step. The lower flight stays where it is today (8 steps, diagonal from the foyer). Instead of a flat landing, one wedge-shaped step cuts the corner and turns the stair 45° to the east, then 5 straight steps carry you up into the kitchen. The island moves about 1.1 m north toward the dining table (about 1.1 m clear between the table end and the island), so the top of the stair opens onto clear floor. All 14 risers are the same 18.3 cm. The upper flight is short, so it stops before the mud-room to garage passage, which keeps about 2.2 m of headroom; the wall tops under the stair (including the top of the bathroom wall) are cut to the stair soffit. Upper floor: the kitchen-lounge wall is back on its original line with its original doorway, and the double-sided fireplace stays in it, and a square walk-in pantry (about 1.0 × 1.0 m, shelving on two walls) sits between the stair and that wall, with its door in the original hall-kitchen wall line; the corner beside it stays open to the lounge with a glass guard over the stair. The wall between the old hall and the kitchen comes out (beam needed) and the old stair opening is floored over. A single 45° winder is normally allowed in a house stair, but confirm with the stair builder and inspector.

Original stair position · One 45° winder · Lands in the kitchen · Garage untouched

19 – Open kitchen

Est. cost $120k–248k CAD · Concept 19 · open in 3D

Concept 14 reworked: full-size island, pantry wall behind the stools, wider patio door, and a TV nook where the fireplace wall was. Concept 14's plan with the current stair. Kitchen: full-size island (2.8 m, four stools) about 1.0 m from the dining table, which sits about 1.25 m from a standard 10 ft patio slider centred on the dining wall; two full-height pantry units and a bar with a beverage fridge on the hall wall behind the stools. The fireplace and its chase are removed and the kitchen-lounge wall is extended from the outside wall, leaving a 1.2 m opening at the stair end, so the way from the stairs to the kitchen crosses only the west end of the lounge. The east end becomes a TV nook: a 75 in TV in the alcove and a big 8 ft sofa with its chaise end against the window wall; the left-most seat faces the TV straight on from about 3.6 m, with about 1.4 m of open floor between the sofa arm and the half wall. The west end, where the fireplace was, is a sitting corner with a window bench. Around the stairwell, the hall-side and north walls are replaced by glass railings with a black top rail (they look bearing, so beams are needed); the half wall between the kitchen and the TV nook is 107 cm high with a 35 cm quartz ledge matching the kitchen counters, overhanging the lounge side for drinks. Ground floor: the under-stair storage gets 1.2 m double doors from the garage (fire-rated, self-closing) as well as its foyer door.

Current stair · Pantry wall + bar · TV nook · 10 ft patio door

21 – Original + pantry

Est. cost $101k–194k CAD · Concept 21 · Latest · open in 3D

The original floor plan kept, with a slim gas fireplace set into the kitchen-lounge wall, a walk-in pantry behind it on the kitchen side, and the concept 20 kitchen. Original walls and stair are kept; the kitchen-lounge doorway loses its door and header and is widened to a full-height opening about 1.8 m wide, from the fridge to the corner cabinets. The corner fireplace chase that stuck about 85 cm into the lounge is removed and replaced by a slim direct-vent gas fireplace (about 38 cm deep) set into the kitchen-lounge wall, flush with the lounge, so the lounge gains that floor. On the kitchen side of the same wall, where the fridge stood, is a walk-in pantry (about 1.2 x 1.7 m inside, its north wall in line with the hallway, shelving on two long walls, pocket door on its north side opening to the hallway) whose back wall encloses the fireplace. The fridge (counter-depth, French doors) moves onto the kitchen side of the kitchen-lounge wall, right beside the pantry. Cooktop in the island at the dining end under a ceiling-hung hood, with the oven/microwave tower directly opposite on the wall run (tall cabinet on one side, coffee station counter on the other). The island and dining table each move toward the other (about 85 cm between them). Two stools at the south end of the island (keeps a clear path between the island and the pantry door); drinks bar in the dining corner. The kitchen window on the wall run is removed.

Original walls · Slim fireplace · Walk-in pantry · Island cooktop · 1.8 m opening
